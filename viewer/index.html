Why pentagons?

You can't cover a sphere with only hexagons. Euler's formula (V−E+F = 2) forces exactly 12 pentagons into any hex-ish sphere grid, like the black patches on a football. This grid is an icosahedron subdivided and turned inside out (its dual), so the 12 pentagons sit where the icosahedron's 12 corners were. Tick the box above to find them.
