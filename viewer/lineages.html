Peoples editor save as world tries
Time runs left to right, Myr to the present, on a log scale (the three scales: tectonic run, glacial time zoom, recent pass). Drag a bar's left end to move when the line appears; click a bar to edit it. Origins: split · root · arrival (from outside) · hybrid · template (overlay). Switching a line off: its split children re-parent to its parent (or become roots); its hybrids and templates go with it. Saved trees: <data>/lineage_trees/; CLI: uv run python -m planet lineages <world> --tree <name>.
