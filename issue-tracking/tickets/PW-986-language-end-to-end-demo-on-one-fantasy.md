## Description
Owner 2026-10-28: an end-to-end language demo in v2, if not the accepted end state. Thin slice through every layer on one fantasy run (L7 world), each layer minimal but real, no stubs faking a later layer:
- demes from PW-981 (the deme tree at ~20 kyr is the language tree);
- minimal PW-982: one proto-language per root deme (small inventory, sonority-sequenced syllables), a short catalogue of conditioned sound changes (~20 rules) and word replacement on a small concept list (core + a few dozen culinary words) run down the deme tree;
- word forms for PW-972's name stacks (endonyms, a few exonyms, rivers/places);
- minimal PW-984: at least one crop or animal word borrowed hop by hop along its spread, nativized at each hop;
- shown in the culinary brief and the peoples workbench (owner uses the viewer), with a cognate table across sister demes.
The owner reviews it as a linguist and rules: accept as the v2 end state (PW-982/984 close at this depth), or keep them later for the full version. PW-983 and PW-985 stay out of the demo.
