## Description
**In plain words.** The owner (10-30) found the dragon-density paper worthwhile as a deep dive: it found real model errors (13 tickets) and gives an outsider a readable account. This epic collects a series of such papers.

Each paper follows the same bar:
- standalone (no file or ticket names on the page);
- cited from PDFs actually read;
- one checker per paper and a scientific peer review;
- a revision, then a re-verify;
- a "what the model should change" section feeding tickets.

The owner picks the next topic; the candidates are listed on the owner's desk page.
