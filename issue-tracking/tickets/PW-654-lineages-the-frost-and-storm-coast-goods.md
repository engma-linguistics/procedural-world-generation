## Description
`frost` (permafrost and the ice's edge) and `storm_coast` (wave power offshore) are built from the finished
world's layers, so like farmland they count only from HOLOCENE_KYR (10 kyr ago). In the glacial epochs the
frost giants and storm giants follow their prefers and comfort ranges alone, although the cold country and
the coasts moved with the ice ages.

A per-epoch frost good is easy from what each epoch already has (temperature and ice: cold ice-free land,
the ice margin), e.g. as a place tag in history._tags; storm coasts would need wave power per epoch (only the
finished world has it). Small; do it if frost giants' glacial ranges look wrong in a showcase.
