## Description
Owner (2026-10-24): beyond hunter-gatherer / farmer / pastoralist. Model subsistence as a way-of-life trait on peoples and groups (cultural, not genetic; groups PW-665): foragers (broad, or specialised and sometimes settled, like fisher-foragers of rich coasts), horticulturalists (gardens, swidden), plough/irrigation farmers, pastoralists (nomadic, transhumant, agro-pastoral mixes), sea nomads. It sets which [[use]] goods a people reads, who tames which animal (PW-718), fuels (PW-722) and food (PW-692). Hill giants as pastoral nomads alongside steppe orcs (a cultural divide; the herder groups' lactase share differs, and fermenting milk (cheese, yogurt, kumis) lets low-lactase herders live on dairy, as in Mongolia). Economies that sit on top of others (raiding and tribute, as fire giants turned warlords might run; trade; peripatetic service peoples) belong to the civilization pass, PW-661; originally such peoples were hunters or herders.
