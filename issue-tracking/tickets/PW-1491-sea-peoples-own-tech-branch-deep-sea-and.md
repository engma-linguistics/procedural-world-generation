## Description
In plain words: Daniel 11-07: merfolk and storm giants end 10-23 kyr behind because the tree's early techs need timber, forage or farmland (scout 206, spread1.md). That's fair for the land ladder, but sea peoples should have their own: heat and light from hydrothermal vents, kelp and shellfish farming, shell and coral tools, pearl and nacre crafts, and whatever leads on to their versions of metal-working, towns and records. Goods come from the sea cells they live in; land peoples can trade for some. Keep it a small branch grounded in what the sea floor offers.
