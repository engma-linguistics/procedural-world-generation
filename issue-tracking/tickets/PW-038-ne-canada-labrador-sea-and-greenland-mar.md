## Description
The Labrador Sea is too warm (~7 °C SST against ~3.5 °C): the overturning's inflow feeds it along the
Labrador coast. SW Greenland and Iceland get summers just above 10 °C, so their Köppen ET becomes D;
Reykjavik's coldest month went from −29.6 to −12.9 °C (observed −0.6). The Earth world's temperature
difference view shows NE Canada, Labrador and Greenland's margins too warm, which also blocks
Laurentide ice inception in the time zoom (Labrador/Baffin summers ~5 °C too warm).
