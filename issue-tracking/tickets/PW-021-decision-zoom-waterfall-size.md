## Description
Zoom falls came out at 140–200 m on rivers of 20,000–60,000 m³/s (tens of Niagaras; 141 m at
60,000 m³/s is ~85 GW, ~70× Niagara), because coarse worlds drop big rivers 500–700 m over a few
hundred km. After `falls2` made falls fit under the coarse ground, big rivers mostly get 50–150 m and
only steep mountain fronts keep 250–550 m: the question became whether taller falls are wanted, which
needs stepped plateau country above the escarpment.
- **A:** keep it.
- **B:** spread the drop over more, smaller steps as discharge rises.
- **C:** fix at the source with more coarse-scale river erosion (outlet incision).

Coordinator's lean was a little B, then C.
