## Description
In plain words: the cosmos has a fixed list of what is good and evil, which detect-evil spells read. Each culture weighs moral
concerns its own way, so a raiding people's war god can be righteous at home and read as evil to a neighbour's paladin.

Settled in the religion doc (https://claude.ai/artifact/Tk4g2nkJPQpTJgeDXo6KoP (Claude Docs id d890d9ce-3695-47c4-add1-a21c3c1a3bf2)), section 4 plus comments. The cosmic ledger is written together with Daniel, from the most
universal agreements (the seven cooperation goods of Curry, Mullins and Whitehouse 2019, plus harm to the helpless). Draft: the
doc's "Cosmic ledger (draft)" tab, 29 acts.
- Per-culture weights on care, fairness, loyalty, authority, sanctity/purity and liberty (Haidt and Graham), drifting with the
  temperament and history of PW-904.
- Each act is tagged by concern, with context words (kin, guest, helpless, enemy, sapient). A culture's verdict = its weights x
  the tags. A god's character is its worshippers' weights; its cosmic alignment = those habits read on the fixed ledger.
- Gate: start once Daniel has settled the ledger tab: its "Your call" rows, plus law/chaos, severity, and intent.
