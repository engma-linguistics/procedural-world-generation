## Description
Owner (2026-10-24): the metals view shows every deposit, far more than any real people would have found. Discovery and working should follow from (1) exposure: depth to the top of the body, erosion and outcrop; (2) surface signs: gossans (rusty caps over sulphide ore), copper stains, placer gold and tin in stream gravels traced upstream, salt springs, oil seeps, coal in river cuts; (3) people nearby and time spent there (herders, farmers digging, well diggers); (4) technology: whether a people can smelt it (copper/tin before iron, zinc and aluminium very late), dig and drain to depth (pumps, explosives), and has a use for it. Store per deposit: known since when, by whom, the share worked. Default view shows known deposits at the target era (PW-690); all deposits stay a debug overlay. Reads the tech tree (PW-675), so it waits for it.
