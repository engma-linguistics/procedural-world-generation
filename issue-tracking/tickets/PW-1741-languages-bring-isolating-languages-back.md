## Description
In plain words: only 5 of 621 living lects are near-isolating, and 2% lack person marking (WALS 22%). The 11-08 heavier-synthesis ruling (FUSE 0.02, L2 strip) pushed every language toward affixes. RULING (owner, 2026-11-11): Daniel wants isolating languages back. Weights only, one lane:
- [ ] a low-synthesis attractor so ~10-15% of living lects stay isolating (Mandarin/Vietnamese-like), e.g. L2 strip and FUSE balance, an analytic drift for high-contact hubs; keep the 11-08 gains elsewhere
- [ ] sex-based gender toward WALS 31A (~33%; ours 8%, non-sex 31% vs ~11%)
- [ ] classifiers persist (ours 2% vs ~35% WALS 55A incl. optional): lower the binding hazard into noun classes
- [ ] polar questions out of the verb-morphology bin (51% vs 17%; particles 24% vs 61%)
- [ ] gram source skew: PAST from YESTERDAY 305/418, GEN from THAT 201/251, NEG from NOTHING 446/446: demote when other sources exist
- [ ] homophonous gram sets (median 4 pairs; d0 xe = CLASS1/2/3/PL/DIST) and zero-form grams setting typology: at least stop glossing identical forms as distinct categories
