## Description
**In plain words.** An idea to note, not decide. Every disaster costs slow breeders more, or for longer, and fast refillers keep what
they lose (lane 73's first-come advantage). So in an eventful world elves and dwarves may simply end up ~2–4% of people. The other way
is to give them more children: elves as "mammal" breeders would grow 0.86%/yr instead of 0.47%; with a fertile window of 100–130 it's
1.09%/yr (life_history.table, lane 85 scratch). That halves their loss to any pulse and stays inside canon ages. The argument both ways
is outlined in docs/review/peoples-events/design.md, section 5.

Write it as a short note (`docs/review/peoples-slow-breeders/`) with cited sources. Test it with the lane 77 suite, events on, slow vs
mammal breeding for elves and dwarves, and read the median shares against 2–4%. Then put the choice to the owner.
