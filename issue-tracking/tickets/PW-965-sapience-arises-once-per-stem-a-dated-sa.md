## Description
Owner 2026-10-28.

**The rule.** Every named D&D kind is genetically modern, with full thought and language, from its start. Pre-sapient time belongs to the stems: the last common ancestors.

**Sapience arises once per stem.** One dated "sapiens" node per stem is where minds arrive. It does not happen at each kind's split: ten dragon kinds evolving minds separately is implausible. The kinds split after that node and inherit it. This extends PW-868's sapient date into a tree node.
- A stem can split shortly before its node from an ancestor that is already big-brained and tool-using. That is the real precedent of Neanderthals and modern humans, about 500-600 ka.
- The non-sapient line may coexist with the sapient one for a time. That is also real: *H. naledi* and *floresiensis* lived alongside modern humans.

**Dragons.**
- The draconic stem holds non-sapient megafauna through the age of dragons.
- At the sapiens node (date to be set, before the chromatic/metallic split at 2.5 Myr) minds arrive.
- The animal line that did not become sapient lives on as **wyverns, drakes and pseudodragons**. These are animal lines, not peoples, possibly counted megafauna for wyverns.
- Dragons spend a long time sapient and numerous without cities: solitary, long-lived predators keep lairs, hoards and lore. That is PW-945's teaching window.
- Peoples' pressure bites hardest at the farming onset (PW-957).

**Real-world timeline anchor** (approximate; the language date is debated):

| milestone | date |
|---|---|
| *H. sapiens* | ~300 ka |
| language capacity | likely by ~135-200 ka |
| full behavioural modernity | ~100-50 ka |
| farming | ~11.5 ka |
| cities | ~6 ka |

**Out of scope.** Giant-line offshoots such as trolls and ogres are not tracked. Anything not in the tree is DM-scale: occasional lone creatures or small bands, too few to count in the simulation. Troglodytes going extinct is fine.
