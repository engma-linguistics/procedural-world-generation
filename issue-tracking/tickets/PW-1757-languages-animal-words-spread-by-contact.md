## Description
In plain words: since lane 20 (PW-1745) a people without an animal gets its word only when the herd reaches its deme (or the wild range covers it). Real languages also pick up the word from neighbours who keep the animal, long before (or without ever) keeping it: traders, raiders and tribute all carry the word. Let contact with a keeping people pass the animal word with a hazard scaled by contact intensity and the trade/elite profile (contact.py), marked as 'known, not kept'; when the herd arrives later, the word is already there.
