## Description
Owner (2026-10-24): the project's literal end goal, the last thing before it is fully usable for its original purpose: culinary ethnography in a fantasy realm. For a region and its peoples: (1) fuels available and how they cook with them (steppe dung for low slow fires; forests with abundant wood; peat, coal, charcoal) from vegetation, soils and resources; (2) what grows and is herded there (domestication epic), fished or gathered (coastal life, PW-188), and what trade brings in; (3) each people's own senses and body (feline peoples not tasting sweetness; bird peoples eating spicy fruit without feeling capsaicin heat) as traits on the tree, shaping its cuisine; (4) its way of life, culture and era (groups PW-665, target era PW-690, civilization PW-661 and tech PW-675). Many steps pay off before it: fuels by region, crops and herds, and senses per people can each come early.
