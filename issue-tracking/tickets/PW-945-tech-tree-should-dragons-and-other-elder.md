## Description
In the v0 tech tree the dragons are an elder kind with no hands. Through `elder_kyr` they come to gesture and language very early, often over 1 Myr ago. But language has `spread = 0`: no people learns it from a neighbour, and each stem comes to it on its own (the owner's "not everywhere at once"). So the dragons know language long before anyone else and teach it to nobody. They end with about 10 techs (speech, lore, burial, ritual, innate magic, hoards), because every tool or craft needs hands. Options:
- A (current): elders are early but alone. The younger peoples find language for themselves (earliest 500 kyr, mean wait ~100 kyr once possible).
- B: elders teach. Language and the ritual and arcane techs pass by contact from an elder kind to a neighbour (a small spread, for elders only), so peoples near dragon country speak first. This gives a myth hook ("the dragons gave us words").
- C: elders teach only the arcane track (druidcraft, elemental pacts, runes), not language.
Lean: C, with B available as a per-tree option (a `teaches` list on the [[kind]]).
