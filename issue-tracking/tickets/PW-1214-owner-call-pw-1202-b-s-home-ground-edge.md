## Description
**In plain words:** lane 84 suggests (PW-1202 B) that a people that has held a land a long time should defend it harder, so that people
arriving by boat make coastal enclaves instead of taking whole continents. You ruled against "first to hold it keeps it forever" and
kept the surge (a growing newcomer's edge). B is a softer form of that rule, so lane 83 did not build it without asking.

Since lane 83 (stage 3: one capacity per people, density by era, fit by breadth), humans already lead fewer continents: 2–8 of 10 are
led by others in screens on world 4360a3, against 1–4 before. See docs/review/peoples-d20/report.md.

Options:
- A. Build B: an incumbency edge that grows with time held, capped (at most ×2), and fading where the newcomer surges. The surge stays.
- B. Leave it. Judge the blocs after the kobold founding fix (the stranded-kobolds draft) and boats (PW-1202 C).
- C. Late deep-sea craft only (PW-1202 C): open-sea crossings from a late date. That cuts the humans' reach rather than giving anyone a
  home edge.

Lean: B, then C. With the stage-3 changes the blocs already appear in some seeds; an incumbency edge risks freezing the map.
