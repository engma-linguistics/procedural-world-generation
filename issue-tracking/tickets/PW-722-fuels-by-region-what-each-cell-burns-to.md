## Description
Owner's culinary end goal (PW-692) item 1, which can come early: per cell and era, the fuels at hand and their shares, e.g. steppe herders burn dung (low, slow fires), forest peoples wood, bog country peat, farmers straw and stalks, treeless coasts driftwood and seaweed, and outcropping coal where a people knows it (PW-714). Inputs: tree_cover/timber, the animals known (herd dung), peat_depth, crops and cropland, resources. Output: a fuel layer (leading fuel plus shares) on the peoples run and on zooms, a viewer layer, and a fuel line in each people's story ('cooks mostly over dung fires').
