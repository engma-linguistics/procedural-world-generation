## Description
In plain words: Daniel 11-07 (D57): besides keeping the even missionary chance, he wants some way to track minority religions in a people or region. Today each culture holds one faith. Record, per culture (and roll up per region/state), the share holding each faith, so a conquered or missionary-touched culture can be mostly one faith with minorities of others, and show it in the Religion tab and culture cards.
