## Description
Owner (2026-10-24): up to ~3 kyr before present most peoples barely intermingle; later, not everywhere is metropolitan, but most areas have 2+ peoples present in abundance and more in pockets (pockets mostly for the zooms and the civilization epic). So a world cell should read like 50% one people, 30% another, ~15% 'other', depending on who is willing to cohabit (from the wishes: near pairs mix readily, apart pairs rarely). Today the run stores each people's presence, a leading people with its share, a mix count and contact hybrids; this story turns the late (~last 3 kyr) presence into a cohabitation-weighted composition per cell, with a cohabitation matrix derived from the wishes plus defaults, and shows it on the globe (leading colour with its share, the second people, the remainder) and in the hover. The zooms place the pockets later.
