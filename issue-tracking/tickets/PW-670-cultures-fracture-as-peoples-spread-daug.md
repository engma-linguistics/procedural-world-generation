## Description
Owner (2026-10-24): as peoples migrate and spread out (because of course they do), they fracture into cultural groups that value different things. A seafaring people landing on a new continent will, hundreds of years later, have the continent's interior peopled by descendants who can barely tell a skiff from a canoe. So a way of life (PW-665 groups) should be inherited along the spread and then drift with the land a branch settles: a founder group's ways carry to its daughter settlements, fade with distance and time away from the land that fits them (sea, steppe, forest), and new ways arise where the land rewards them. Seafaring share by exposure to the coast (PW-666) is one case of this. Not genetics; no warfare or displacement here (also PW-661).
