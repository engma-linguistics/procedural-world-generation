## Description
Owner 2026-10-28: before the natural dragon density is fixed, show it on maps once prey varies by country (PW-966). Use dots or circles per dragon, sized or coloured by stage. Tie the counts to areas a person can picture:

| reference area | km² |
|---|---|
| Washington DC | ~180 |
| a county | ~2,000 |
| Wales | ~21,000 |
| California | ~424,000 |
| France | ~550,000 |

The owner's hunch, to compare against rather than to fit: "California has maybe 2 ancients at a time, ~10 adults and 30+ young", not "37 dragons in a DC-sized patch". Show counts under several age schedules (base, younger, harsher; PW-958), not one.

Show:
- the natural density (the age of dragons, before peoples) and the present (pressed) side by side;
- counts by stage, wyrmling, young, adult and ancient (PW-958);
- per kind and per landmass;
- what dragons eat against what the peoples nearby raise. In aggregate it is small, but locally a single ancient taking ~100 cattle a year ruins a valley, which is where adventurers come from.

Format as PW-923's report: under a page, mostly pictures.
