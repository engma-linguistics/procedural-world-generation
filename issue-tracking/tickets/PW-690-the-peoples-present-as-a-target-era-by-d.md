## Description
Owner (2026-10-24): by default, climb population, spread, intermingling, civilizations and technology to around the vague renaissance era D&D worlds default to. Today the present is simply the run's end; the '1000 AD' mentioned on 2026-10-24 was only an Earth comparison for ~300 M people farming 2-3 Mkm2, not a target. Earth anchor for a renaissance-like present: ~450-500 M people around 1500 AD, ocean-going ships, horses and plough agriculture widespread, large states over much of the farmland. Options: A) the target era is a setting on the tree (default 'renaissance') that sets the end state the history climbs to (densities, boats and horses available, unifying pressure); the years of history stay as the tree gives them. B) simulate forward in time past the present until the target is reached. Coordinator's lean: A; the tree's split times already fix the calendar, and without the tech model extra simulated years would only add population, not an era.
