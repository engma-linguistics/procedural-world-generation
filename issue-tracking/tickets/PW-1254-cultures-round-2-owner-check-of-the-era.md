## Description
In plain words: three of the new numbers are judgements. On your dnd runs they give 111 to 130 living cultures: humans 28 to
32, kobolds 22 to 24, storm giants 6, and one per dragon kind.

1. How long a cut-off part must stay apart to become a new culture: 600 years in the stone age, 450 in the bronze age, 300
   classical, 200 medieval, 150 renaissance. The two ends are sourced. Speech parts into separate tongues in about 500 to 700
   years. Settler colonies were told apart in 120 to 175 years. Your premise that the time shortens with tech is partly
   right: it holds for crafts and tools, but not for speech, which changes at a steady rate in any era. The steps between
   the ends are a judgement.
   - A (lean): keep the table.
   - B: use one value for all eras (150 years), since speech shows no trend by era.
2. Cohesion: dragons 0.9, giants 0.5, everyone else 0.
   - A (lean): keep these values.
   - B: give some peoples a little cohesion (elves, dwarves: long-lived and conservative).
3. Sister cultures of any people living side by side merge after 1,000 years (sooner for a cohesive kind).
   - A (lean): keep. This is what holds the count down.
   - B: 2,000 years, which gives more cultures (humans would be in the 40s).
