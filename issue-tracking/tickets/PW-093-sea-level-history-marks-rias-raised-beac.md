## Description
From the time zoom's sea-level curve: drowned river valleys (rias) and estuaries where the last rise
flooded valleys cut at lowstand; raised beaches and terraces where land rebounded; shelves exposed at
glacial lowstands (land bridges like Beringia), recorded as a world layer and drawn in the zoom.
