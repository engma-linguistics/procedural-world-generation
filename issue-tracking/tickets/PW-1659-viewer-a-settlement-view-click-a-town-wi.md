## Description
In plain words: owner 2026-11-10: clicking a settlement opens a view (popup or tab) with what we know about it, more than the hover card's one line:
- Names: the English Common name, each local name with its meaning and who gave it when, older names (PW-1654, PW-1656, PW-1658).
- People: tier and population over time, peoples and shares, founders, changes of hands, wars and walls, ruins under it.
- Economy: goods it makes and buys (crops, herds, mines, trade), from the settlement and food data where they exist.
- Rule and faith: the state and its governance style once polities exist (PW-1549), the faith(s) held there.
- Language: the lects spoken, Common or trade language in use.
Show what is there now, hide empty sections; grows as the other systems land.
