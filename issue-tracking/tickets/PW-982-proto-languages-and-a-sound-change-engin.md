## Description
Owner 2026-10-28. The product path ('start faking it' at ~20 kyr, where nothing older is recoverable anyway). Builds on the deme tree (PW-981): demes that share an ancestor at the start share a proto-language; sister proto-languages drawn independently stand in for deeper relatedness (seed shared regional features deliberately).
- Proto sound inventory: PHOIBLE-weighted, feature economy, vowel dispersion, constrained by each species' anatomy (body keys: beaks, muzzles, size; signing peoples).
- Syllable shapes and phonotactics from the sonority sequencing principle.
- Diachronic from day one: a catalogue of attested, context-conditioned sound changes weighted by naturalness (Index Diachronica style) applied along the deme tree; core-vocabulary replacement at realistic rates on a concept list (core + culinary concepts); meanings that merge with CLICS-style colexification; vocabulary elaborated where the sim says life needs it (herders: milk words); new words by compounding/derivation with recorded etymologies.
- Grammar only to the depth output shows: typology features drawn from Grambank/WALS correlations, drifting at known rates.
- Fills the empty word forms of PW-972's name stacks.
Checks (plausibility, not parity): inventory sizes and segment frequencies PHOIBLE-ish; an automatic comparative method run on the output should go blind somewhere near 6-10 kyr without that being set.
- [ ] related demes have recognisably related words; the reconstruction horizon emerges
- [ ] peoples, places and foods have word forms with etymologies
