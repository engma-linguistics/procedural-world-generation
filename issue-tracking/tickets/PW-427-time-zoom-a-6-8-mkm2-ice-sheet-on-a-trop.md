## Description
From the 2026-10-16 ice-extent analysis. 080df3 (made before PW-275) grows a 6.8 Mkm2 sheet at about 0-15S, 90W at its 126 kyr maximum and again near 918 kyr. Its median thickness is 2.6 km on a bed ~1.6 km high, which looks like elevation feedback running away. Earth's tropical plateaus held no sheet at the LGM. Both cold time zooms also keep 1-2.8 Mkm2 of ice below 25 degrees latitude, against tens of thousands of km2 on Earth at the LGM. That is borderline, given 4-5.5 Mkm2 of tropical land above 3 km and global means of 7-10 C. Re-check on a fresh time zoom after PW-366 lands; if the sheet still forms, review the low-latitude elevation feedback.
