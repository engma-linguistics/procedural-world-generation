## Description
The Antarctic margin makes only ~2.5 Sv of bottom water (AABW observed ~10–20 Sv): the Weddell and Ross
seas are too fresh (~34.0 psu against 34.4–34.6). Their winter brine is weak, and there's no shelf /
polynya "ice factory". Check the brine from wind-driven sea-ice export (offshore winds push new ice
away, so more forms and more brine is rejected).
