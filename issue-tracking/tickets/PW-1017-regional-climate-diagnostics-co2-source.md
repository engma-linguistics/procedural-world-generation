## Description
**In plain words.** Today's recorder gives the planet's totals: global CO2 in and out, global temperature, each ice sheet's budget. When a change freezes or cooks worlds, the totals say that it happened, not where. This would split the CO2 sources (ridges, arcs, hotspots), the weathering sink, erosion supply and ice by continent, at a few snapshots ~20 Myr apart and around a cold or hot excursion, so one region or subsystem going wrong stands out. Owner 10-29 suggestion; build it when a diagnosis needs it (likely the coasts icebox question, PW-998). Read-only, like tectonics/diag.Recorder.
