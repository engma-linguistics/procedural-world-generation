## Description
In plain words: every culture is named "[country] <people> of <place>". On a big continent several human cultures end up
with the same name and get a number: on 622c00 the name "temperate humans of the south of Gugiki" is used seven times. Each
culture needs a name of its own. Cheap steps: name it after its founding river or landform and its founding date ("humans
of the Kesh river, 12 kyr"). Later, a gloss name from PW-972's endonym patterns. `cultures.document` builds the name.
