## Description
Owner (2026-10-24): a cheap first pass toward the culinary ethnography epic (PW-692). Like the existing flying or swimming traits, each people gets culinary-relevant senses and body traits from real-world analogues, set in the tree editor (checkboxes or a dropdown) with D&D-preset defaults: can taste sweet (cats can't), digests milk as adults (lactase persistence), feels capsaicin heat (birds don't), and a few more (bitter sensitivity, umami, smell acuity, obligate carnivore, cellulose or raw-meat tolerance). Shown in the run's notes and stories; nothing reads them yet besides the future cuisine pass.
