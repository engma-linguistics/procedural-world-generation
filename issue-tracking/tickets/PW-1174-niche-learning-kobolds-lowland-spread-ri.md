## Description
Evidence for the single-origin ruling (lane 77's SPLIT_FOUNDING, PW-1152). Measured by lane 79 on world 4360a3, dnd, seed 7,
one try.

With today's founding ("range"), kobolds split 350 kyr ago and inherit the draconic stem's range. With niche learning they then
learn the temperate lowlands before the humans (200 kyr) and keep part of them:
- LEARN_GAIN 1: kobolds 6.5% -> 27% of people;
- the shipped 0.6: 6.5% -> 11.6% (10.2% at seed 10, 10.3% at seed 13).

Under SPLIT_FOUNDING = local, kobolds are 0.1-0.2% with or without learning. Dragonborn are likewise 3-4% under range and 0.3%
under local.

So the kobolds' gain under learning is mostly a head start on an inherited range, not learning as such. If the owner picks local
founding, re-check that the minor draconic peoples are not lost outright: 0.1% is a remnant. That is a founding question, not a
learning one.
