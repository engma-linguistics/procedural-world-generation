## Description
Owner 2026-10-28 ('great catch'). Real analogues: tea/chai, tomato from Nahuatl. The part of historical linguistics the culinary book actually prints. When a crop, animal or dish reaches a people through trade or crop spread, its word comes along from the giver's language, reshaped to the receiver's inventory and phonotactics, relayed hop by hop (each hop's sound changes and truncation, the doc's X4 pattern). Borrowings form datable strata; conquest leaves substrate words. Needs word forms (PW-982) and the crop/trade spread history.
- [ ] the brief shows, per food, the word, where it was borrowed from and along which route
