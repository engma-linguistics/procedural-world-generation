## Description
Let a run specify its moon(s): mass/size, distance, and several moons (fantasy settings). Tides are the sum of each moon's tide (scales with mass / distance cubed) plus the sun's, giving spring/neap patterns from moon phases; very large or close moons also stabilise or destabilise the axial tilt (tie to obliquity in the time zoom). Viewer: show the moons' parameters with the tilt indicator.
