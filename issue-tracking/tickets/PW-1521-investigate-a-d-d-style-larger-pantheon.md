## Description
In plain words: Daniel 11-08 asks for an investigative lane. Not a world where the winners' religion takes over, but one where, in some regions, a larger pantheon forms over time: each kind of people (dwarves, elves, orcs) keeps or gains its own gods, and the major domains (war, sea, harvest, death) get gods of their own, worshipped across peoples. Regional, not worldwide. Investigate how real pantheons accreted (syncretism, interpretatio, Hellenistic and Roman pantheons, Hindu traditions) with sources, and how gods.py's beings, domains and fusing could support it. A design write-up plus, if cheap, a prototype read-out on one run.
