## Description
**In plain words.** Today a village, town and city are fixed sizes: 300, 2,000 and 10,000 people at any date. The owner (10-30) wants the size classes to follow real-world settlement sizes as they changed through history. What counted as a city 2,000 or 5,000 years ago may have been far smaller than a city now, or maybe not.

The sizes must come from sourced archaeology and urban history, not memory: the largest settlements by era, typical town sizes, and rank-size patterns. This is a good candidate for a deep-dive paper (see the papers epic), whose findings set the thresholds per era and per tech level.
