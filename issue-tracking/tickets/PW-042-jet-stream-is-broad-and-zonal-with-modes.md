## Description
The thermal-wind jet (`jet_e`/`jet_n`) is broad and mostly zonal. Earth's polar-front jet is narrower and
meanders around land–sea contrasts and mountains (e.g. the Rockies/Tibet split). Sharper temperature
gradients or a terrain term would help; the jet also steers the storm tracks, so check rain.
