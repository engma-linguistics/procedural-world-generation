## Description
(Belongs beside the civilizations epic PW-856; an epic cannot sit under an epic.)

In plain words: every culture gets gods and spirits that come from its land and its way of life. They split, merge and change as
the peoples do. Each culture also gets food taboos and its own sense of right and wrong, set against a fixed cosmic good and evil
that spells can detect.

Settled with Daniel in the religion doc, https://claude.ai/artifact/Tk4g2nkJPQpTJgeDXo6KoP (Claude Docs id d890d9ce-3695-47c4-add1-a21c3c1a3bf2), rounds 1-2 (2026-11-01..02):
- Mode switch: faith-made by default (a god exists once worshipped, and its power follows its faith). Belief-only and absolute
  are options. No miracle feedback into the history in v2.
- Gods form a tree like lects: born in a deme, split, merge (syncretism), drift and fade.
- An individuation slider runs from a place-bound spirit to a person-god. Only beings above a threshold grant spells.
- Faith steps sit on the tech tree's social track.
- Two moral ledgers: a fixed cosmic one (Daniel and Claude are writing it together in the doc's ledger tab) and per-culture weights.
- Dragons' gods are emergent and likely the oldest.
- First slice: the god tree, individuation and food taboos as a readout. The morality act list comes after the ledger is agreed.

Children (drafts filed alongside): gods tree, faith tech nodes, food taboos, moral ledgers. Coordinator: PW-907 (gods
placeholder) is probably superseded by the gods-tree story. Re-parent it under this epic or fold it in.
