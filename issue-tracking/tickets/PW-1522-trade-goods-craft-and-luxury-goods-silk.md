## Description
In plain words: Daniel 11-08. The tech tree already holds silk, cotton, linen, wool cloth, purple and indigo dyes, incense, glass and glassblowing, porcelain, lead and silver mining, and the whole ladder of weapons and armour; but trade's 20 goods are mostly raw (grain, salt, ores, spices, gold). The goods worth carrying very far (Silk Road: silk, spices, incense, glass, silver) are largely made goods, where only some places have the know-how. Add them: a good made where its tech and raw input are, in towns (specialists), precious or middling by value; silver separate from gold. Literature first (which made goods travelled how far, when). Then the long-haul network should read as a branching network. After lane 218 merges (trade.py).
