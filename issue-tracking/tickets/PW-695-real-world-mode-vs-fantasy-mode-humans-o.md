## Description
Owner (2026-10-24): one switch for how a world is peopled and furnished. Real-world mode: humans only (the tree's human line and its groups), real resources, Earth's domesticable animals and crops. Fantasy mode: the D&D tree (or a custom one), the fantasy resources (PW-607), non-Earth domestications (PW-691) and, later, magic. Stored on the world or run, honoured by planet lineages, resources, domestication and the viewer's toggles.
