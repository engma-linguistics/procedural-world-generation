## Description
Bottom of the barrel, way post-v1 (owner 2026-10-20). Once Earth-like planets are easy, push the simulation to exotic worlds: snowball/frozen planets, hypervolcanic or partly molten worlds, hyperfecund (life-saturated) worlds, moons of a gas giant (tidal heating, eclipses, giant-planet tides), and fanciful gas giants with floating continents. Design principle carried from now on: derive thresholds and calibrations from each planet's own quantities (orbit, sunshine range, heat flow), never as hard-coded Earth numbers, so these worlds don't need special cases (first applied in PW-440's ice-age thresholds). Related: PW-219 (other sizes and densities), PW-216 (moons).
