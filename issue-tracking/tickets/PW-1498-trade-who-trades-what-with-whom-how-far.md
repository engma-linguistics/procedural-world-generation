## Description
In plain words: Daniel 11-07: 'Something I'd love to see, and can absolutely have its own lane: some trading. Starting with a literature review and outlining what types of trading by what kinds of people over what time periods... then implementation.' Today trade exists only as a trait (techtree.trade_of, insularity) that speeds writing along trading towns (D53), plus forager-farmer exchange notes (PW-1405). Phase 1: a sourced review (gift and reciprocity exchange among foragers, down-the-line exchange of obsidian/shell, forager-farmer exchange, pastoral-agrarian exchange, long-distance luxury and caravan trade, maritime trade, markets, money, merchant diasporas; by kind of society and era, with distances, volumes and goods). Phase 2: a design for the model (goods from the world's own resources/crops/herds, routes over the map, who carries it, what it changes: tech spread, food, wealth, wars, faiths, the culinary brief). Phase 3: implementation in small steps.
