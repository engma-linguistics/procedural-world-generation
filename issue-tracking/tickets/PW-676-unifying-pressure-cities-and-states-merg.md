## Description
Owner (2026-10-24), on PW-670's fracture: the counterweight is merging and melding. Cities, then states and civilizations, impose a unifying pressure on their peoples. Real-world spread: tribal and very small groupings persist to a point (the many First Nations of the Americas; Europe's small states and distinct identities around 1000-1500 AD, the Holy Roman Empire; ~20 Bantu languages around Kilimanjaro), but as a culture's technology expands a unifying presence follows (Rome, modern large states). So: fracture by distance and land (PW-670), merge by the reach of states, which scales with technology (the tech epic).
