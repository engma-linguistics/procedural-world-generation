## Description
In plain words: many real names honour someone: saints and gods (Welsh Llan- + saint, Spanish San/Santa, Beth-El 'house of god'), rulers (Alexandria, Constantinople, the Soviet -grad renamings), or copy a homeland ('New X': New York, Nova Scotia, Nieuw Amsterdam). Conquerors rename towns and the old name often comes back (Istanbul, Volgograd/Stalingrad). Use the faiths (gods.json) for holy names and the states (PW-1549) for rulers, conquests and colonies; record the rename chain so the history is visible.
