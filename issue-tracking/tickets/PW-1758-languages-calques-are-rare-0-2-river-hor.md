## Description
In plain words: when a people meets a new animal or thing, real languages often translate the donor's word part by part (hippopotamus 'river horse', German Fernseher, Finnish rautatie 'iron road') instead of borrowing its sound. Lane 20 (PW-1745) added calques to wild_step and tech_step, but they come out at 0-2 per world for the horse. Raise the calque share where the donor word is a transparent compound, and a receiving lect's contact is long and bilingual (high l2), as real calquing follows bilingualism.
