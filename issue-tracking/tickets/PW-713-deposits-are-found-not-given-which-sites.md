## Description
Owner 2026-10-24: the metals view shows far more deposits than any real people would have found. Each people should hold a 'known' share per deposit, from: exposure (outcrops, gossans, river placers and stream-sediment trails are found early; buried deposits need tech: shafts, geology, later geophysics); proximity and time lived nearby; what the people needs and can smelt (no tin hunting before bronze; iron ores ignored until bloomery smelting); and trade and word of mouth spreading knowledge. A second share is how much of a known deposit is worked, capped by tech (depth limits from drainage and ventilation, the grade worth working falling as tech rises) and demand. The viewer's metal layer would then show 'known to this people / worked' instead of everything in the ground. Fantasy metals (PW-607) take the same path; arrivals may bring knowledge with them. Depends on the tech tree (PW-675) and the resource layers (PW-609/618).
