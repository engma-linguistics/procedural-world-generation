Real-world analogue: the Moon formed much closer (tens of thousands of km) and Earth spun faster (days of a few hours early on; ~21-22 h at ~600 Ma per tidal rhythmites and coral growth bands). As the Moon moved out, tides weakened and the day lengthened.
