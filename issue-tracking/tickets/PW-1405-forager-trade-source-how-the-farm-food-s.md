## Description
In plain words: the foragers' trade share (PW-1386) has a sourced cap (about half their food, Congo Basin), but the point where it is
half that cap (5 farmers per km2 within reach) and the reach (one ring of cells) are judgements. The only relation found in the
sources read is distance: Aka camps 3-4 hours' walk from the village ate more farm food than camps 5-8 hours away (Bahuchet 1988, as
reported by Kitanishi 1995 p. 107).

Not read in lane 146 (paywalled, or no open copy found): Bailey & Peacock 1988 (Efe; Kitanishi's Table 11 uses their Table 5.5 and
Fig. 5.2), Bailey et al. 1989 American Anthropologist 91: 59-82, Hart & Hart 1986 Human Ecology, Headland & Reid 1989 Current
Anthropology 30: 43-66, Bahuchet 1988 itself, and the Human Ecology 19(2) 1991 forum. One of them may give the share by camp distance
or by village density (the Efe and their Lese villages, the Agta and their lowland farmers).
