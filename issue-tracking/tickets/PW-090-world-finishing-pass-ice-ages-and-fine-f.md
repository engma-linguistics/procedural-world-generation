## Description
Owner (2026-10-14): worlds aren't toys; they're the basis for a world he'll *use*, e.g. for a game. At a
chosen point in a run, run the ~1 Myr glacial time zoom so an ice age or two comes and goes as the timing
happens to fall, with glaciers actually carving valleys. That becomes the world's "last phase" finish.
The epic also collects the finer-grained features that spruce up a world for real use.

**First step: PW-091** (time zoom from a chosen world, feeding back into a finished world). Then the
glacial imprint (PW-037) and its zoom synthesis (PW-092), sea-level marks, river detail, soils,
wind features, coast detail, resources and hazards, human-use layers, export, and zoom microclimate.
Subsystems: timezoom, zoom, surface.
