## Description
In plain words: peoples now split into cultures. A part that took to new country is one at once, and a part apart for
1 kyr or more is one too. Sister cultures merge again under cities or written law. Each dragon kind is one culture, along
colour lines (Daniel, 2026-10-31: sapient, but no fragmented cultures for now). On your dnd runs that gives about 135–155 living cultures today
(360–370 over the last 300 kyr): humans 36–40, kobolds 26–30, dragonborn 11–22, kenku 12–15, storm giants 13, most other
peoples 1–5. Two calls for you, in the Cultures tab of the workbench.

1. Giants. They are megafauna in the model but sapient in D&D. Storm giants have 13 living cultures, and cloud, fire and
   frost giants have many short-lived ones.
   - A (lean): keep their cultures.
   - B: one culture per giant kind, as the dragons are.
2. Is the count right?
   - A (lean): keep it. 1 kyr apart is about the time speech takes to part into separate languages, plus some margin.
   - B: fewer cultures. Raise the time apart to 3 kyr, or merge at chiefdoms too.
   - C: more cultures. Drop to 0.5 kyr.
