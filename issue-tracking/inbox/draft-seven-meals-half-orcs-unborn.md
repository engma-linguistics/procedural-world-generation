## Description
**In plain words:** in Seven Meals the half-orcs are a mixed people of humans and orcs, born where the two meet, from 10 kyr ago.
In one run of the stress suite on lane 83b's code (world af2677, seed 13) humans and orcs never met, so there are no half-orcs. In the
other nine runs they make 19–38% of all people. Humans were small in that run (9.8%).

Measured: suite-final.md (sm-s13@af2677: half_orc 'never founded'; also gold dragons and fire giants lost, as in base runs).

To look at: is this D20's one-spot founding (humans and orcs born far apart, and no boats crossing in time), or the hybrid's date? A
preset people that is a hybrid could be founded where its parents come nearest, once they have both been alive long enough, instead
of waiting for them to meet.
