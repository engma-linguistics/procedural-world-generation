## Description
How v0 handles language:
- It needs gesture, which is possible from 3 Myr.
- Ordinary peoples can reach it from 500 kyr (Dediu & Levinson 2013 argue for Neanderthal speech).
- It comes at a rate of 0.01 per kyr (mean wait ~100 kyr) times a size factor, and never passes by contact.
- A split inherits it only if the parent had it at the split.

So on a typical D&D roll the stems reach language between ~500 and ~150 kyr ago. Now and then a people is still without full language at the present. It keeps gesture and call systems, and every craft that needs language stays blocked. Options:
- A (current): leave it. A rare people without language is a fantasy hook.
- B: no living people of the peoples tree may lack language by the present (raise the rate in the last 100 kyr).
- C: later and tighter (earliest 200 kyr, the common reading that only modern humans had it).
Lean: A, with the report counting peoples without language.
