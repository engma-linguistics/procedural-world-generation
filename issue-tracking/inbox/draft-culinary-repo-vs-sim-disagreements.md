## Description
Per PW-734 the world owns the map, so these are for the culinary side to adopt or to reword (run e63bfd). Each is
a fact of one world and one seed; re-read after any re-run.
- **The fiends are small.** Antelope-kin hold ~2.6 Mkm2 (about 2.5 million people in five variants) and cervid-kin ~17 Mkm2, against 81 Mkm2 of humans. The
  "empire" that is routed around is a minority of rulers, as the intake proposed, but on this world even the cervid-kin are a minor people. The sim found no
  conquest by either (substrate takeovers between the two are ~80-100 kyr old); the 2.1 kyr and 3 kyr conquests are authored `[[event]]` anchors.
- **Humans and the fiends mix heavily** (33.7% and 25.6% mixed cells with humans) with no gene flow (T3). The canon's Jangwa point (three clades, no hybrids)
  holds for the fiends too.
- **Goliaths hold ~11 Mkm2 in 9 patches** at 0.15 per km2 (many patch-fadings under the viable number): the "far north" is a world-wide cold belt here.
- **Merfolk and the deep folk are coastal** (no marine habitat yet): the deep folk are on island and coast cells (0.8 Mkm2), not the Deeps.
- **Humans are one 81 Mkm2 people with a margin forager root covering almost all of it**: the forager group's niche is broad (the canon puts it
  at the steppe margin only). The group map, not the preset, is the thing to read; tightening the group's niche to the margin is a preset edit if the owner wants it.
- **Half-elves and culture-only elf groups are not modelled** (draft-half-elf-no-population-switch), so the brief cannot say "same land as" for the two elf cultures.
