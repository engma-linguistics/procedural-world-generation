## Description
In plain words: in the review, obsidian and flint are the classic hand-to-hand goods (Renfrew's fall-off, review1.md section 1). The
world has volcanic ground, which the goods ledger reads as obsidian, but it has no flint or chert, which come from chalk and limestone.
So the ledger lists who has spare obsidian, but not who lacks edge stone: without flint, nearly every culture would lack it.

A flint layer would let step 2 move obsidian to the people who lack good stone. It could come from sedimentary carbonate rock in the
world's geology, if the world has such a layer. Low priority: metals and salt carry the ledger's story.
