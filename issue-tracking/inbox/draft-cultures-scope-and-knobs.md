## Description
In plain words: peoples now split into cultures. A part that took to new country is one at once, and a part apart for
1 kyr or more is one too. Sister cultures merge again under cities or written law. On your dnd runs that gives about
140–165 living cultures today: humans 36–40, kobolds 26–30, dragonborn 11–22, most peoples 1–5. The dragon kinds also get
cultures: up to 67 that ever lived for one kind, mostly short-lived, though only 1–2 are alive now. Two calls for you,
in the Cultures tab of the workbench.

1. Dragons and giants (megafauna stems).
   - A: keep them. A dragon "culture" is a regional population with its own country.
   - B (lean): skip the dragon kinds (solitary, no culture to speak of) and keep the giants.
   - C: skip both.
2. Is the count right?
   - A (lean): keep it. 1 kyr apart is about the time speech takes to part into separate languages, plus some margin.
   - B: fewer cultures. Raise the time apart to 3 kyr, or merge at chiefdoms too.
   - C: more cultures. Drop to 0.5 kyr.
