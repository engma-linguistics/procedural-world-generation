## Description
In plain words: the lexicon and contact reviews ranked what real vocabularies and real contact situations have that
the generator lacks. The before-merge items are done (borrowing by meaning and intensity, numeral systems, colour
stages, kinship systems, sound symbolism, substrate, areal copying, technology words, taboo and avoidance speech, gods
and liturgies, segment borrowing). Polities have their own draft. This is the rest. If never done, the world has no
pidgins, creoles, mixed languages or trade languages, every language has one parent, and vocabularies differ only in
form, not in how they build words.

From contact-gaps.md §6 (rank):
- [ ] 8 shift timing: speed-up past the tipping point (~0.3), old speech decays over a few generations unless an enclave
  holds it (rates are in the words/contact decisions draft)
- [ ] 9 two L2 regimes (short-term adult L2 simplifies; long-term child bilingualism copies categories), direction by
  prestige
- [ ] 10 dialect continua: same-language neighbours borrow core words at an intensity-weighted rate; asymmetric
  intelligibility
- [ ] 11 vehicular languages: an L2 variety forked from a widely used lect; "the Common" as the largest
- [ ] 12 diglossia (H/L) once writing is known (shares machinery with the liturgies)
- [ ] 13 mixed languages (Michif, Media Lengua) for merged or mixed-descent peoples (`crossing.py`); needs `parents` as
  a list in the true tree
- [ ] 14 pidgins and creoles in mixed cities with no majority lect
- [ ] 16 exogamous multilingualism (Vaupés)
- [ ] 7b a culture's tolerance (`attitudes.py`) scales its loan share; low tolerance turns loans into calques

From lexicon-gaps.md §6:
- [ ] 7 per-lect derivational inventory ('-ness', action noun, privative, 'having', female, collective, ordinal) and a
  richness draw, so Hungarian-like and Mandarin-like lexicons differ
- [ ] 9 an ideophone class (sounds, textures; usable in recipes)
- [ ] 10 frames of reference (absolute/relative/intrinsic; SEAWARD, UPHILL ...)
- [ ] 11 calques (donor's compound recipe with native parts); 13 herd and crop practice words travel with the route
- [ ] 14 lexicon size by way of life (no HUNDRED in a restricted-numeral lect; the sketch shows the gap)
- [ ] food routes: the hop table and the receiver's etymology disagreed in 27% of hops (11-03 handoff); re-measure,
  then show both or mark the hop as superseded
