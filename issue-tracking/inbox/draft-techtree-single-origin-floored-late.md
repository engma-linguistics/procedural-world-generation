## Description
In plain words: bread was baked first about 14,400 years ago on Earth, and in our worlds the first bread now comes about 9,700
years ago. Once someone has it, it spreads to most peoples. The tree doesn't know the real precondition (people gathering wild
grain intensively), so it just waits for a lucky people.

PW-1138's rule (lane 70) gives a floored tech with two or more dated Earth origins the band's rate. Bread, wild grass-seed
harvesting and fermentation have one origin or none in the catalogue, so they keep the slow track rate (0.02-0.03 a kyr). Reach
is fine now because contact is faster (bread 62% -> 72% of living peoples, fermentation 62% -> 74%, beer 0% -> 73%; ~72% is
the ceiling across the tree in these runs), but the world first is late: bread 9.7 kyr (median over 4 runs x 2 draws, Earth
14.4), fermentation 5.3 (13), beer 4.7 (13).

Try: the real precondition as a need (bread and beer on intensive wild cereal use: wild_cereal_harvest with grinding slabs and
storage, the Natufian package), then the soft-anchor rule applies above the floor; or the catalogue's second origins, if the
source hunt finds them (pass 2).
