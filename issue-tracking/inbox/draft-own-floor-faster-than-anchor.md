## Description
**In plain words.** D55 asked for a floor: a people alone should climb no slower than a set rate. Lane 200 set writing's floor from
its four independent inventions (Wikipedia, History of writing): a lone people with towns and proto-writing writes within a median
4.35 kyr. But the tree's own rate for writing is already faster than that for a big people. The anchor's first of 4 peoples writes
in 3.3 kyr, Earth's lag. With the floor on, the anchor writes in 1.86 kyr and the anchor test fails. So the floor is off. A floor
should catch only the weak learners: one set at a slower, "worst Earth region" wait (Mesoamerica or China after its towns, which
needs fetched town dates per region), or one that caps the boost so the anchor is unchanged. Separately, on the humans-only world
the climb now stops at clay tokens (2 of 8 draws with region learners). The catalogue's tokens and proto-writing origins are
marked unverified, so they need a fetched source before they set any rate.
