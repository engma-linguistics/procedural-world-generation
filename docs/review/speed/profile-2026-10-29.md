cProfile of `planet simulate --seed 4242` on main (0ca89abc), Threadripper, default threads (16 numba, BLAS 1). The lanes' sims were
SIGSTOPped, but lane 26b's 5-process scorecard kept running until partway through (it paused itself later). So the load was ~20 at the

The climate runs every `climate_every` = 4 Myr, and at every step of the finish window. Each update costs ~1.3 s at L6 and ~4.9 s at L7.

Hot spots at L7 (self time):
- `climate.carry`: 39 s, 1320 calls of ~30 ms. It is a fixed `km / h + 10` sweep relaxation, ~150 sweeps at L7 and ~75 at L6. It is
  already `prange`, and it starts from scratch at every call.
- `climate.rain`: 22 s.
- `climate.currents`: 16 s.
- `climate.smooth` and `_coarse_smooth`: 22 s.
- `month_temps` and `months`: 17 s.
- vegetation `equilibrium` and `potential`: 19 s.
- `hydrology.route`: 8 s.
- `icemass.balance`: 8 s.

## Where speed could come from (not yet tried)
1. **Climate on a coarser grid during the spin-up.** L7 climate costs ~4x L6's per update. Running it at L6 outside the finish window
   would bring an L7 run to roughly half or a third of its wall time. Each L7 world's own climate is still computed at L7 in the finish
   window. Output-changing: it needs the paired judge, and the owner's L7-first rule (the spin-up's erosion, weathering and ice would
   be driven by an L6 climate).
2. **Warm-start the relaxations (`carry`, the ocean's `_carry`, `smooth`) from the last update's field, with a convergence test in
   place of the fixed sweep count.** Fewer sweeps per call. Output-changing at the convergence tolerance only.
3. **An adaptive climate cadence:** skip an update when the land and sea and the ice have barely changed since the last one.
   Output-changing.
4. **Bit-identical work:** the Python-level numpy in `rain`, `currents`, `month_temps` and vegetation (~80 s of self time at L7) into
   numba. This is the PW-247 kind of gain, probably 1.2-1.5x.
