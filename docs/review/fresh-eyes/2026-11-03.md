# Fresh-eyes review, 2026-11-03

A read-only review by an agent with no prior context, asked by Daniel on 11-02 for a holistic view and next steps.
It changed nothing. Citations are the reviewer's; "opinion" marks its judgement. The coordinator's take is on the
Owner's Desk (D44).

## 1. Overview
The project is a careful planet simulator with a fast-growing peoples and culture layer on top (`planet/` 64.7k lines,
`planet/lineages/` 17.0k, 138 commits to lineages in the last week). The physics is mostly sourced, honestly labelled and
well guarded (939 tests, golden tests, a two-tier pass bar, three strikes working: lanes 119, 120, 121, 125, 131, 132 merged
as docs-only diagnoses). The October audit (`docs/review/code-audit-2026-10/index.html`) covered code hygiene.

Risks: the end goal (the culinary ethnography) is behind: breadth grows faster than the payoff, the brief still answers
"where is this species" not "what does this culture cook", and languages, polities and religion are queued ahead of food.
The peoples layer reads its climate inconsistently. Plant growth is load-bearing for both climate and peoples. The board
grows far faster than it closes.

## 2. Recommendations, ranked
- **R1. Per-culture brief and a cuisine sheet v0, before languages and polities.** Cultures are the unit of the book
  (decisions 10-25, PW-856) but `brief.build(run_ref, region, people, era_kyr)` (`planet/lineages/brief.py:248`) takes no
  culture; cuisine synthesis is a few if/else notes in `_suggested` (`brief.py:1099-1150`, `"drink": None`). The culinary
  repo's five questions (fuel, staple, drink, who eats together, what outsiders get wrong; `culinary-dnd/README.md`): drink
  and who-eats-together have no model. Cultures r2 puts the brief last (`docs/review/cultures-proposal/proposal-r2.md` §7).
  Fold in PW-1312, PW-905, PW-744; move `_suggested` to `cuisine.py`; regenerate or delete the brief_version 7 samples in
  `docs/review/lineages/showcase-peoples/briefs/`. Effort M.
- **R2. One environment per epoch for the peoples.** Habitats read each epoch's climate (`history/habitat.py::_land_view`);
  the density ladder reads the finished world's NPP for every epoch, glacial ones included (`ladder.py:126-127`); herds read
  the world's grazing NPP (`prey.py:3`). PW-1077 measured 843 cells with world rain >600 mm but epoch rain <200 mm.
  `HOLOCENE_KYR = 10` (`history/params.py:69`). One epoch-environment provider (even NPP rescaled by epoch climate); fold
  PW-1077 in, link PW-902. Effort M.
- **R3. Plant growth as a shared contract; cut the weathering's tie to it.** Grass 1.4-1.8x too high and wet tropics ~0.55x
  cancel in the global total that `EPS_SCALE` is tuned on (`docs/review/vegetation/npp-check/diagnosis.md`, PW-1331);
  fixing grass warms L6 3-5 C via weathering (PW-1332); lane 140 failed the hard bar. The same field sets people density and
  herds. Opinion: PW-1332 B (weathering's plant factor from cover or roots, not raw NPP), then land PW-1261 and PW-1331 once.
  Effort M + one judge.
- **R4. Board under control.** 11-02: 90 created, 34 closed; ~65/day created vs ~25 closed since 10-23; 689 open; v2 384 ->
  447. 266 open tickets under "timezoom" because there is no peoples subsystem (`issue-tracking/README.md:97`). docs/review
  119 MB, 1,446 of 1,468 files kept by refs. Add peoples/culture/food subsystems; sweep stale decision tickets; observations
  into the lane write-up, ~3 tickets per lane at most (opinion); PW-900 workgroups as epics or close it. Effort S-M.
- **R5. Move the physics judge out of review folders.** `tools/hard_bar.py:22-23` imports `passrate4` from
  `docs/review/base-climate/v1-finish-12`; `pjudge.py` lives in `v1-finish-16` (`docs/coordination.md:25`). Move to
  `tools/judge.py` with a test. Effort S.
- **R6. Binary world layers (PW-1209) before more L8/land-zoom work; sweep at 2-4 km, not 1 km.** L8 worlds are 440-452 MB
  JSON; an L9 world 1.76 GB (PW-1262); a 1 km section ~110 GB. PW-1360 says 4 km/px suffices for peoples. Effort M.
- **R7. A ledger of peoples mechanisms before polities (refactor: needs a yes).** About a dozen stacked mechanisms
  (overview §2.26), 85 constants in `history/params.py`; some do nothing at defaults (share bands: neither dnd.toml nor
  seven_meals.toml sets `band_pct`; `disease_resist` read by nothing; hunts are map story only). Measure each on the lane 77
  stress suite and retire the near-zero ones. Effort M.
- **R8. The Holocene clock is Earth's.** `gate_kyr = 11.6` (`samples/techtree.toml:40`), `HOLOCENE_KYR = 10`, but "now" is
  the latest interglacial's sea-level peak (`finish.py:86-89`). Check on showcase time zooms; if it varies, read the gate
  from the world's own last deglaciation. Effort S for the check.
- **R9. Peoples in `planet make` and the New planet form.** The make chain stops at zooms (`make.py:5-14`); no ticket.
  Peoples runs still stored as `kind: timezoom, phase: lineages` (`lineages/output.py:161`). Effort S-M.
