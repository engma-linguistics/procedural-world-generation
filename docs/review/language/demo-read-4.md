# Demo read 4 (grammar D demo, published as version 4, 2026-11-08)

Full read; text dump with line numbers at scratchpad/review4/demo.txt. demo-read-3 items fixed except where listed. To fix at the cause:

**Pronouns and homophony (lexicon + grammar)**
- Polite-address notes say "the plural is used for you" but sg = pl in kenku, frost giant, elf, dwarf, dragonborn pronoun tables. A lect whose 2SG and 2PL merged can't use the plural politely; check before drawing T-V, or renew the plural.
- Pronoun mergers that break sentences: kobold 1SG = 1PL.EXCL, 2SG = 3SG; storm giant 2SG = 1PL.EXCL ("hear us" = "hear you"); firbolg 2SG = 1PL.EXCL. A merger veto (or renewal) for core pronoun cells is needed.
- Numerals: dragonborn hundred = ten, three = five; kobold eleven = fifteen; dwarf forty = two. Families-card numerals differ from the grammar's numerals (blue, green dragon, human, elf). Who = what (dragonborn).
- Kenku book spellings "null", "inull" (small, child) read as a bug; avoid spelling a word as "null"/"none"/"undefined".

**Notes vs forms (grammar/sentences/demo)**
- "Merged/fused" labels where the form is bare or transparent (kenku see.2PL.OBJ; kobold go.3SG; frost giant PRF+1SG; dragonborn; dwarf go.PRF).
- "The verb carries the subject's 3SG marker" when only class concord shows (frost giant, kobold, dragonborn, storm giant); frost giant "3PL marker" form u-.
- Far demonstrative "reinforced by a place word" but form ≤ 'that' (kenku, storm giant).
- Classifier descriptions vs use (drow CL2 'people' on dog/house/milk; human CL3 'tools' on dog/fish; storm giant CL2 'abstractions' on man/dog; kenku CL1, kobold CL1).
- Dwarf split-aspect ergative never appears in its past sentence; dwarf ACC merged form differs between sections; vowel ä missing from the chart; remote-past note garbled ("PST.REM (nearer to further back)").
- Two stem shapes for one noun (frost giant dog; kobold dog; kobold setaxe with unexplained x).
- Green dragon copula clitic inside the postposition phrase (boat=COP with).
- Unlisted allomorphs (green dragon DAT =we; dragonborn LOC -ju); kobold 'with' free word in one line, COM clitic in another; dragonborn "clitic after the verb" sits on the first word.
- Event line vs table form (Aliy'unun 2SG.POSS /ŋɨ̞n/ vs /ŋɨ̞ŋ/).
- "N families of 3+ words" cards list 2-word families; kobold reuses the human proto-form for 'belly'. Weak felt families (ox, grandfather, burning).

**Texts**
- Myths: the same faith ("sky faith of the earliest hill giants…") told by drow and by elves with different gods/domains/gift — one faith should give one pantheon (then each lect names it); explain why a people holds another people's faith (conversion) or pick myths only for a faith's own holders. All six myths use the same wild-swine taboo — vary by the faith's actual taboos. Gods take INAN/"things" or "abstractions" class: gods should be classed as persons. Frost giant invocation doubles the object clitic (=1PL.OBJ=1PL.OBJ); god name equal to a grammatical marker (Moow). Odd marks in god names (D̮ï, Nị́dérrìnù, Ṭíghùwísì); capitalisation of book lines differs by language.
- Recipes: "Take some alliums. Grind it…" (number agreement); "on the fire" glossed 'in'; hyphens inside roots in book spellings (irh-ll, h-yipihyeng) look like affix breaks.
- Fable: proto is ~20 kyr from the daughters, so few cognates survive (bird, tree, come, water, good, eat, 2SG all replaced). Pick a shallower ancestor (about 3–6 kyr) with 2–4 daughters so the reader sees sound change on shared words; say how old the ancestor is.
