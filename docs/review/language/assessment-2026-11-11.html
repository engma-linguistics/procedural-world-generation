Language Subsystem Assessment

Independent read of planet/language/ and run a24559 (621 living lects), with a fresh de1cc6 pass as the second world. 2026-11-11, main @ 02050888. Five slice reviews plus my own measurements; evidence at the bottom.

As a set of mechanisms: yes, pretty much done. Every piece a historical linguist would ask for exists, runs from real reference data, and most of the earlier gap reviews are now stale. Nothing structural is missing for this project's use.

As output a linguist reads: not yet. About ten cheap, visible defects sit on the first page: a pronoun borrowed in 10% of languages, "the land of the named after the the river", crows called "the yellow one", "rr" in a third of place names, no isolating language anywhere, living sound systems that drifted away from the natural ones they were drawn as.

What it takes: four short fix lanes (about a week of lane time) plus the names and viewer lanes already planned. A handful, not a long tail, provided the tail is parked on purpose.

Main risk: everything was calibrated on one world. de1cc6 fails 2–3 of the 17 checks on every draw, and 6 of the 17 measure the generator's own inputs.

2. Ranked realism gaps

Avisible to a user of the world   Bvisible to a specialist   Cinvisible in the output

  1. ACore vocabulary borrowed too often. "I" is a loan in 10% of lects, ONE–THREE in 16–22%, MOTHER/FATHER in ~20%; 38 of 52 "I" loans come from the paradigm-repair step, which borrows before it derives and ignores borrowability. Why here: the etymology pane is the thing a reader opens first, and this is the first "that's wrong".
  2. ALiving sound systems are unnatural as systems. No /m/ in 15%, no plain coronal stop in 13%, retroflex sonorants without obstruents in 11%; series shares far from PHOIBLE after 20 kyr although protos matched; half the lects run on one vowel for >40% of vowel tokens (fiɲaɲimi, xejɛxɛrɛ), and /i/ not /a/ is the commonest vowel in half the world. Why here: every word and every sounds table; same on de1cc6, so structural not seed noise.
  3. ANo isolating languages, little gender, no classifiers. 5 of 621 lects near-isolating; 2% lack person marking (WALS 22%); sex-gender 8% (33%); classifiers 2% (35%) because every classifier binds into a noun class within 1.5 kyr at FUSE 0.02. Why here: flavour range. No Mandarin-like, no Vietnamese-like, no Romance-gendered Elvish on offer.
  4. ATaboo paraphrases are random and over-applied. CROW 'the yellow one', DOG 'the red one', BEAR 'the long one'; the hazard also fires on pigs (114), cattle (69) and fish SCALE (126). Why here: hunting and herd words are culinary core. Slam dunk.
  5. AString bugs in names and the Common. "the land of the named after the the river" (62), "Market The Black Sea", "Loyings 2…5" (530 numbered names), "the A languages"; living families and languages unnamed ("true family p306", "fam0"). Why here: on the map labels and the Families tab. Slam dunk.
  6. ARomanization quirks. "rr" written for every trill even in the 501 lects with no tap; three spellings of one morpheme on one page (khe/che/yrhaènrhè); 61-letter patronymics; only 6 given names per lect and no way to ask for more. Why here: a DM needs names in bulk, and they all look faux-Spanish.
  7. BFlat family structure and a lumping scholars' tree. 175 isolates of 264 families (Earth: isolates are ~3% of languages); largest family 34 lects; scholars' families right for 44% of lumped pairs; horizon 14–17 kyr because cognate share plateaus at 0.12–0.25 from 7 to 15 kyr; 549/579 languages single-lect, so no dialect level; intelligibility 0.08 for unrelated pairs. Why here: the Families tab reads as a world of isolates. Partly the peoples simulation's doing (no expansions).
  8. BSound-change churn and saturation. Re-fires are 43% of a lineage's events, 11% land exactly 4.0 kyr after the last (REFIRE_KYR); 37 rows fire on ≥80% of lineages; founding bursts of 12–72 changes. Why here: history lists look mechanical. Lane 17 owns the cause (PW-1692).
  9. BHomophonous and zero grams glossed as distinct categories. Median 4 homophonous gram pairs per lect; 31 of 260 class systems have identical class markers; 286 functions survive only as zero forms yet set the typology ("split" alignment with ERG = '').
  10. BNames don't age and feature names are formulaic. 232 of 233 own-line settlements read exactly as coined; 203 peaks from 6 adjectives; 46% of lands 'the land of the real people'. Already ticketed (PW-1685, 1656).
  11. CSpecialist-only tail. Undirected semantic shift paths (10-step FIRE…MOUNTAIN→HIGH chains), no variation phase or synonymy, no calques, no suppletion, static stress, no pitch accent, no syllabic consonants, English sentences are a template not a translation. Nobody using the world will see these.

6. Risks

  • Single-world calibration. Every constant in calibration.md was set on a24559. A fresh de1cc6 pass fails sisters (0.493), horizon (14.0) and living sizes (no lect over 42 consonants). All 8 recorded de1cc6 passes fail at least one non-horizon check; overuse fails 6 of 8, pronoun loans 4 of 8. Making HUNDRED/THOUSAND 3× more borrowable flipped three checks.
  • Likely lane-16 regression on de1cc6 sisters: 0.476–0.513 in 4 of 5 draws since lane 16, against 0.53–0.55 before; status.md calls it "accepted".
  • Stacked calibrated multipliers interact (PULL_BOOST Q 3.0, FUSE 0.02, L2_STRIP cubic, PRE_FUSE 0.1 …): the Q fix overshot into verb morphology; the synthesis fix eliminated isolating languages; _adopt is a one-way ratchet toward more marking. Dragons (430-yr generations) run outside the calibrated range.
  • Six tautological checks make "16 of 17 pass" read stronger than it is; the areal check passes when there are no pairs; the doublet bar is far below the output.
  • Catalogue sinks without sources (aspiration, ts, ɾ, ŋ) are structural, so every world drifts the same way; de1cc6 is worse (prenasals 61%).
  • Hand-written tables repeat motifs world-wide: RAIN→water 2711 times, PERSON→man 2592, ROOF→house 2495; one fixed list of 10 English phrase-name templates; 6 peak adjectives.
  • Engineering: an absolute checkout path in constants.generation_overrides.spec breaks byte-identity across worktrees; no test that worker count leaves output unchanged; benchmark baseline is stale (pre-lane-14) so "0 WORSE" means little; places_page.py and sisters_page.py hardcode lane-15 text.
