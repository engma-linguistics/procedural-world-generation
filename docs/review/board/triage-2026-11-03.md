# Board triage, 2026-11-03 (lane 156)

## In plain words

Daniel said the project is getting too fiddly and wants "reasonably stable and not prone to exploding when nudged" as the north star, with a backlog instead of one huge v2. So I cut v2 from **382 open tickets to 89**. Nothing was thrown away: 291 tickets moved to `later` (the backlog), each with a one-line reason in its Updates. Two were closed as clear duplicates or superseded. Every in-progress and needs-decision ticket stayed where it was. The five biggest judgement calls, which Daniel may want to reverse, are listed at the end.

What v2 now holds: the culinary ethnography (what each people eats, cooks and burns), the few things that make a planet or a people visibly broken (worlds that finish too warm, snowball spells, a hard-failing cold planet, peoples that stay tiny remnants), the whole-planet land zoom already in flight, and the speed pass. What left: rain-kernel calibration, regional temperature polish, zoom polish, viewer niceties, ore and salt-mining detail, languages, polities, religion, war and plague, and the provenance project. All still written up and findable.
