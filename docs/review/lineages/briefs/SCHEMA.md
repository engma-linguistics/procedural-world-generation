# Culinary brief schema (brief_version 5)

`planet brief RUN --region lat,lon[,km] | --people ID [--era KYR] [--format md|json]`, or `--all [--eras 0,0.5,...] --out DIR`
(one brief per people and group at each era slice, and per mixing zone at the present, into `DIR/<run>/`). The JSON is the
contract (`planet/lineages/brief.py`, `BRIEF_VERSION`); the Markdown is a reading of it. A key that is `null` is not built yet
(listed under `not_modelled`), never a zero.

"Era" column: **era** = read from the frame nearest the era asked; **present** = the run's last frame whatever the era;
**world** = the world's own slice moment (climate, soil, rivers, coast, goods, resources), not the era; **run** = a run-wide fact.

## Run output behind the contact record (PW-727)

`meta.contacts` of a peoples run: `pairs` (every two peoples whose ranges touched at a stored epoch: `a`, `b`, `tier`, `split_kyr`, `first_contact_kyr`, `years_in_contact`, `in_contact_now`, `border_km_now`, `border_km_peak`, `mixed_pct_now`, `kind`, `series`), `substrate` (per people, as in the brief), `tier_kyr` (the tree's `meta.tier_kyr`, default 10 kyr, 1 Myr, 40 Myr). Tiers: `split_kyr` is the older of the two branchings below the lines' common ancestor (a parent and its child: the child's age); no common ancestor or an arrival from outside: T3.

The body-share layers (`body_<trait>`, % of a cell's people) carry a cline across the border of two T0/T1 peoples: the share diffuses about 300 km into the neighbour's side (`contacts.cline`, output only: the wishes, the ranges and the empty land are unchanged).

## Run output behind reputation, group ranges, fuels per era and routes (PW-731, PW-742)

- `meta.present.reputation` = `{params, rows: {observer: {subject: row}}}` (`planet/lineages/reputation.py`, built from `meta.contacts` and the tree at the end of a run). `params` holds the knobs in force (`efold_kyr`, `era_scale`, `min_strength`, `loss_scale`, `rich_years`, `thin_years`, `thin_km`; `meta.reputation` in the spec overrides them). Groups are folded into their people; only peoples with land at the present are listed. A conquest memory under `min_strength` is gone and the pair reads by its contact. Not read yet: authored conquests and polities, derived incursions (PW-661): there is no "resented" (ruled by) valence.
- The folk categories are data: `[[exonym]]` rows in the spec (`id`, `label`, conditions `body = {key = value}`, `traits` (any), `ancestor`, `origin`, `way`, optional `valence` list and `efold_kyr`). The row with most conditions met wins for a subject; a row with only a valence is the fallback for that valence. `planet/lineages/samples/dnd.toml` ships a generic table (devils for horns, demons for antlers, giant-kin, sea elves, fae, the goblin menace, and the fallbacks); which peoples are called what in a particular world is its rows and bodies (PW-734). A row's `efold_kyr` (> 0, kyr, not scaled by the era) is its own memory length, a legend: a conquest remembered under that row fades with it instead of the global 1.5 kyr x the era's scale, and the reputation row then carries `memory_kyr` (absent for every other row; PW-754). `samples/seven_meals.toml` (the owner's world, PW-730) gives its devil (horns) and demon (antlers) rows 30 kyr. Bodies also carry `display_sex` (`both` | `males`: which sexes carry the horns or antlers; the story notes say "in males only"), beside `display` and `shed`.
- `layers/groups_best.bin`, `groups_best_pct.bin` (per frame): the group with most presence in the cell (index into `meta.lineages`; 255 none) and its presence in percent.
- `meta.eraq` and `layers/eraq_<dens|farm|herd|wood|herd_mult|farm_mult>.bin` (frames within 12.5 kyr of the present; `eraq.frames` are their frame indices): the land use's inputs at the frame (`landuse.frame_inputs`) for the fuels per era.
- `layers/route_<stock>.bin` (present, one per animal and crop; `meta.animals/crops.stocks[id].route_file`): 0 not known, 1 origin, 2 road, 3 sea.
