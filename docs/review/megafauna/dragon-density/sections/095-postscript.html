Postscript: what changed after this analysis

This paper describes the generator as it stood when the runs were made. Its findings on where dragons live (Section 5.3) were acted on soon after. Three dragon kinds were given open country: gold dragons now live on warm grassland, and blue and copper dragons on cold steppe. Dragon kinds also gained a middle tier of habitat, land they tolerate without preferring it. And a population can now spread into nearby empty, rich land within about 300 km of where it already lives.

A paired test of 12 runs, with the generator before and after the change, measured the effect. The share of rich empty land held by at least one dragon kind rose from 52% to 90% (on that test's own runs and measure, so not directly comparable with the 76% of Section 5.3). The generator's own checks on the peoples did not change. The numbers in this paper, including the game-present table and the Sword Coast counts, are those of the generator before the change: wild grassland, and the richest empty land in general, would now hold more dragons than they show. The other findings, on the food chain, the pressure of the peoples and the size of a viable population, are untouched by it.
