The ranges are the peoples' real ranges from the run (the layer of who holds most of each cell), at 13 stored moments; the map shows the moment nearest the slider. Land a people holds is lit when that people knows the tech by then; grey land is held by peoples that do not yet; dark land is empty. Stars mark inventions; arrows run from giver to taker (by contact or shared). Equirectangular, 1° pixels.
