# PW-1556: a guard that L7's climate stays as arid as its nested L6 climate (scout 232)

**In plain words.** The test `tests/test_nest.py::test_the_l7_climate_is_as_arid_and_as_wet_as_its_nested_l6_climate_on_one_ground`
(marked slow, ~70 s) builds seed 21's L7 ground without running any tectonics, computes its climate on L7 and through
`nest.climate_coarse` on L6 (what an L7 run's spin-up does), and checks that the arid share (Koppen B over ice-free
land) differs by under 2.0 points and the land rain by under 5%. On today's code it is +1.0 points and -0.2% rain; on
the 10-15 rain kernel that caused PW-290 it would be +5.9 points and -32% rain, so it fails there as it should.

**The failing check.** 6926583's `planet/` (`git archive` into a scratch dir) has no `nest.py` (PW-247 came later),
so the 10-15 kernel was run on the same saved seed-21 ground on L7 and on the L6 cells point-sampled (the nested grid's
first n6 cells, scout 221's `old.py` approach). Today's code on that same comparison: +1.39 points, rain 0.987.
The 10-15 code: **+5.86 points, rain 0.681.** Both limits catch it by a wide margin.

## How the tolerance was chosen

- Arid share: 2.0 points, the ticket's figure. Today's spread over seven unrun grounds is +1.0 to +1.7, so the chosen
  seed sits 1.0 point inside it and the worst seed 0.3; the regression it guards against is +5.9 here and +6 to +8 on
  scout 221's runs. A tighter limit (say 1.5) would leave seed 21 0.5 points of room for ordinary rain-model changes;
  a looser one (3) would still catch the 10-15 gap but not half of it. 2.0 keeps the ticket's number and over 1 point
  of room.
- Land rain: 5%, the ticket's figure. Today within 1.1% on every seed (seed 21: 0.2%); the 10-15 kernel -32%.
- Seed 21: the ticket's seed, the smallest residual of the seven, and an arid share (35%) with room to move both ways
  (the test also asserts the L6 share is between 20 and 60%, so a broken ground can't pass by having no deserts).

Harness (scratchpad, not kept): `pair.py` (the table), `old_pair.py` + `old.sh` (current vs 6926583, point-sampled).
